# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in ScriptKit a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with other people
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission. In this project that includes teams' private report links and
  programme data.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities and scope

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take
fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and will explain their reasons when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[PRINCEdevon](https://github.com/PRINCEdevon), using the contact options on their GitHub
profile, or by a private vulnerability report (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) if no other
private route is available. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy
and safety of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding the consequences of a breach:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behaviour: a private,
   written warning explaining what happened and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may
   be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning, with no
   interaction with the people involved for a set time. Breaking these terms may lead to a
   temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a
   temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from the project.
